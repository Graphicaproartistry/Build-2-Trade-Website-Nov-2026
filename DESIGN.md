# B2T website: design rules

## Non-negotiables
- **Must not look AI-generated.** Professional, restrained and smooth.
- **No neon:** no glowing lines, coloured glows, gradient text, glowing borders,
  purple or blue "tech" gradients, or sci-fi HUD elements.
- **Orange is used selectively:** CTAs, active states and key details only, never as a section fill.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| B2T Orange | #FF9D2E | Primary accent. Text on it is always navy. |
| Deep Navy | #101827 | Dark sections, nav, footer |
| Soft White | #F5F7FA | Light sections |
| Electric Blue | #2563EB | Links and info on light surfaces (use #60A5FA on navy) |
| White | #FFFFFF | Cards, type on dark |
| Orange ink | #A65400 | Orange *text* on light surfaces (AA) |

## Dark vs light
- **Dark:** hero and laptop, product showcase and dashboard demos, full-width construction
  footage, feature storytelling and transitions, final CTA and footer.
- **Light:** reading-heavy content (pricing detail, FAQ, blog).
- **Dark look:** navy, a neutral overhead light falloff, large white type, orange highlights.

## What keeps it from looking AI-made
- **Real material:** genuine product UI and photographic footage. No abstract "tech" filler.
- **Typography does the heavy lifting:** strict grid, generous space, editorial scale.
- **Motion has a reason:** it explains the product, with physical easing and no gratuitous effects.
- **Glass and blur sparingly:** glare is a subtle, realistic material highlight, never a glow.
- **Plain copy:** no "unlock / supercharge / revolutionise" language.
- **Icons:** one consistent line icon set. No emoji and no 3D clip-art blobs.
