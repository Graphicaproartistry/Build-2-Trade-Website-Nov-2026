## 4. How it works page

**Headline:** From first quote to final invoice
**Intro:** Build2Trade follows the way a real job runs. Here's what happens at each step, for each person on the job.

### Step 1: The builder posts a project
The builder adds the project: title, address, start date, description and plans or photos. Then they choose the trades they need (for example Plumbing, Electrical, Carpentry) and the sub-trades inside each one, with a scope, a budget and dates for each trade.

**Two ways to publish:**
- **To your network first:** send the project to tradies you already know and trust, for a set number of days.
- **To everyone nearby:** every tradie with that trade within their working range is notified.

### Step 2: Tradies send quotes
Tradies see projects that match their trade and area. They read the scope, check the plans and send a quote with their price, GST choice, what's included and any documents.

Changed your mind on the price? While the quote is still waiting, the tradie can edit it. The builder sees exactly what changed.

### Step 3: The builder compares and hires
All quotes for a trade sit side by side. The builder picks one and hires the tradie in one tap. Everyone else is told the job has been filled.

If the builder changes the project after quotes come in (more work, a new sub-trade, a bigger budget), every tradie who quoted gets a notification and an email showing exactly what changed. They can update their quote or keep it as it is. Every change is recorded.

### Step 4: Schedule and work
Hired trades appear on the project calendar with their start and end dates. Everyone gets a reminder before work starts. Chat keeps the conversation in one place.

### Step 5: Materials
Builders and hired tradies can ask suppliers for a quote on materials: the items, quantities, delivery address and the date they need them. Suppliers quote, the requester accepts, and the supplier tracks the order through to delivery, with deposit and final invoices.

### Step 6: Invoice, pay and review
When a trade is finished, the tradie sends the invoice through Build2Trade, with GST worked out. The builder pays the tradie directly. Both leave a review, which builds trust for the next job.

## 5. For Builders page

**Headline:** Run every job from one place
**Sub-headline:** Get quotes from the right tradies, keep every trade on schedule, and know what each project is costing you.
**Buttons:** [Sign up as a builder] [Get the app]

### The problems we fix
- Chasing tradies by phone and text for quotes.
- Quotes on paper, in emails and in messages, impossible to compare.
- "That wasn't in my quote" arguments after the scope changes.
- Not knowing what the job has cost until the end.

### What you get

**Post once, get the right quotes**
Add your project with trades, sub-trades, scope, budget and plans. Build2Trade sends it to tradies who do that trade within range, or to your own network first.

**Save as a draft**
Not ready to publish? Save the project as a draft and finish it later, on the web or the app.

**Add, change or remove trades any time**
Add a trade to a live project and the right tradies are told straight away. Remove a trade nobody has quoted on yet.

**Your network first**
Have tradies you trust? Invite them by phone or email and give them first look for a set number of days. If nobody picks it up, publish it to everyone with one tap. You can also "publish again" later to remind tradies about trades that are still open.

**Bring your team with you**
Invite the tradies and suppliers you already work with to join Build2Trade by phone or email, and see who has joined.

**Compare quotes side by side**
See every quote for a trade together: price, GST, inclusions and documents. Hire in one tap.

**Every change recorded**
If you change the project, tradies who quoted are told exactly what changed. If a tradie edits their quote, you see the old and new price and their note. Every version is kept with the date and time.

**Calendar and reminders**
Every hired trade sits on the project calendar with its dates. Reminders go out before the project and each trade starts.

**Expenses and receipts**
Log every cost against the project and trade: materials, hire, labour and more. Attach receipts and invoices. See totals by trade and for the whole project, and export the project's expenses as a report.

**Permits in one place**
Keep track of the permits for each project and who issued them.

**Suppliers and materials**
Find building suppliers by trade and area, with their reviews. Send a material quote request (items, quantities, delivery address, needed-by date), compare quotes from different suppliers, accept one, and track the order through to delivery with deposit and final invoices.

**Supplier marketplace**
Browse products from building suppliers by category, see prices and sales, save products and add them to a quote list. Send the list to the supplier for a quote with delivery to your site.

**AI that saves you hours**
- **Set up a project from your plans:** AI reads your plans and suggests the trades, sub-trades and scopes, ready to check in the Create Project form.
- **Take-off and material list:** a material list with quantities from your plans or photos, ready to send to a supplier for a quote.
- **Compare quotes:** pick a project and a trade, and AI compares every tradie's quote, explains the differences and suggests the best fit. Results are saved so you can open them again.
- **Expense from a receipt photo:** snap a receipt or tax invoice and AI fills in the expense for you to check.
You check and approve everything. AI never publishes or saves on its own.

**Your dashboard**
A home screen with your projects, quotes waiting, hired trades and costs at a glance, plus a checklist to finish setting up your profile.

**Chat, notifications, reviews**
Message tradies and suppliers about the job. Get push notifications and emails for new quotes, changes and invoices. Review tradies when the job is done.

- **Can I invite my own tradies?** Yes, by phone or email, before the project goes public.
